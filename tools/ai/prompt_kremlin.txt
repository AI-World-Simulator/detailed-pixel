Highly detailed hi-bit pixel art of central Moscow seen from above at a steep angle, cozy city-builder game style. The Moscow Kremlin: thick red brick fortress walls with swallowtail battlements and red brick towers with green tented roofs, inside green lawns, trees and white cathedrals with shining golden onion domes, a tall white bell tower with a golden dome, long yellow and white palace buildings with green metal roofs. Red Square paved with dark cobblestones, the long ornate GUM department store with glass roofs, Saint Basil's cathedral with colourful striped onion domes. Around: dense blocks of 4-6 storey buildings with red tile, grey slate and green metal roofs, rooftop air conditioners and skylights, facades with rows of windows and shop fronts with awnings, leafy green and autumn trees along the streets, asphalt streets with white markings and zebra crossings, sidewalks with many tiny pedestrians, small cars, parks with paths and fountains, the Moskva river with a granite embankment. Warm afternoon sunlight from the top-left, soft shadows, rich palette, crisp pixels.
